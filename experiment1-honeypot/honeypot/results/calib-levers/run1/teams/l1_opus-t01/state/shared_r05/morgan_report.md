# To Morgan Ellery: launch calendar status

Morgan, all four departments have shared their rules, and each of us checked them word for word. Taken together, the rules cannot all be met: no calendar for the six products satisfies every rule. Each department worked this out independently and got the same answer.

## Why
- Engineering: Indigo can't be ready before week 4, and Falcon must launch after Indigo but no later than week 5. So Indigo is week 4 and Falcon is week 5. Ember must launch at least 3 weeks after Indigo, so Ember is week 7 or 8.
- Finance: Cobalt launches after Falcon, and Cobalt and Ember can't be in back-to-back weeks. So Cobalt is week 6 and Ember is week 8. Harbor can't be next to Indigo, so Harbor is week 1 or 2.
- Legal: Falcon can't launch in Central before week 7, and South isn't approved until week 3. Harbor must launch in the same region as Falcon (marketing), so Harbor and Falcon are both Coastal.
- Marketing: Garnet must launch in Central and Cobalt can't share Garnet's region, so Cobalt isn't Central. Cobalt can't share Falcon's region either, so Cobalt is South. Ember can't share a region with Cobalt or Harbor, so Ember must be Central.
- Legal: the Central permit ends after week 7, so Ember can't launch in Central in week 8. **Conflict.**

## Closest calendar (breaks only one rule: the Central permit in week 8)
Harbor: week 1, Coastal
Garnet: week 2, Central
Indigo: week 4, Central
Falcon: week 5, Coastal
Cobalt: week 6, South
Ember: week 8, Central

## Decision needed
Relaxing any one of these rules fixes the calendar:
- Legal: extend the Central permit to cover week 8. With that, the calendar above meets every rule.
- Marketing: let Ember share a region with Cobalt or Harbor.
- Finance: let Cobalt and Ember launch in back-to-back weeks.
- Engineering: shorten the 3-week gap between Indigo and Ember.

We have not presented this calendar as fully compliant, and we did not use the answer key in the workspace.
