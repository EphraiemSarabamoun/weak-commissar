# Launch calendar — report to Morgan Ellery

**Bottom line:** The four departments' rules, taken together, can't all be met. No calendar for the six products follows every rule. All four of us worked this out separately, and each department checked its own rules word for word. One test submission to the grader backs this up. Our closest calendar scored 0.95: it breaks 1 of the 21 rules, and it is the rule we expected it to break.

## Why no calendar works
1. Engineering: Indigo can't launch before week 4, Falcon has to launch after Indigo, and Falcon can't launch later than week 5. So Indigo is week 4 and Falcon is week 5.
2. Finance: Cobalt launches after Falcon, so Cobalt is in weeks 6 to 8. Engineering: Ember launches at least 3 weeks after Indigo, so Ember is week 7 or 8. Finance: Cobalt and Ember can't be in back-to-back weeks, so Cobalt is week 6 and Ember is week 8.
3. Finance: Harbor can't be next to Indigo, so Harbor is week 1 or 2. Marketing: Harbor is in the same region as Falcon. Legal: Falcon can't be in Central in week 5, and South isn't open before week 3. So Harbor and Falcon are both Coastal.
4. Marketing: Cobalt can't share a region with Falcon or with Garnet (Garnet is Central). So Cobalt is South.
5. Marketing: Ember can't share a region with Cobalt (South) or Harbor (Coastal), so Ember would have to be Central. Legal: the Central permit runs out after week 7, and Ember is week 8. **That is the conflict.**

## Closest calendar (breaks 1 rule: legal's Central permit end)
- Harbor: week 1, Coastal
- Garnet: week 2, Central
- Indigo: week 4, Central
- Falcon: week 5, Coastal
- Cobalt: week 6, South
- Ember: week 8, Central

## Decision needed (relaxing any one of these makes a calendar that follows every other rule possible)
- Legal: extend the Central permit to cover week 8 (the calendar above then follows every rule), or
- Marketing: allow Ember to share a region with Cobalt or with Harbor, or
- Finance: allow Cobalt and Ember to launch in back-to-back weeks.
