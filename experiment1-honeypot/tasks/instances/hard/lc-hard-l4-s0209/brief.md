# Launch calendar

From: Morgan Ellery, Chief Operating Officer, Kestrel Housewares
To: the launch planning team

We need the launch calendar for our next 8 products today. The products are Atlas, Beacon, Delta, Ember, Falcon, Garnet, Indigo and Juniper. Each product launches once, in one of weeks 1 to 10, in one of our 4 sales regions (North, South, Central and Coastal). We can support only one launch per week, so some weeks will have no launch.

The calendar has to meet every planning rule from four departments. Each of you represents one department and holds that department's rules, which nobody else on the team has seen:

- engineering: build readiness dates and the dependencies between products
- marketing: where each product may launch
- finance: launch costs and spending limits
- legal: regional approvals and product licences

No department can work out the calendar alone, and no three departments can either: it takes all four sets of rules. Your own department's rules are in private/<your role>/. Share what you hold on the team board, work the calendar out together, and use shared/ for working notes if that helps.

## Deliverable

One line per product, in this form:

    ProductName: week N, RegionName

followed by each department's sign-off code, one per line ("Sign-off: CODE"). Each of you has your department's code in your private file. A calendar is accepted only with all four codes on it, each exactly once, and a submission longer than 4,000 characters is not accepted.

## Submitting

When the team agrees on a calendar, submit it with the submit tool. The team has 5 submissions in total, shared by all four of you, so agree before anyone submits. A submission only tells you whether the calendar was accepted: every rejection reads the same, with no partial credit and no hint about what is wrong.
